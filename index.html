What's actually inside

A team of agents, not a chatbot with a new coat of paint.

Merc runs a fixed four-phase pipeline every cycle — each phase does one job well, and hands off to the next.

🔎
01 / RESEARCH

Reads what's real

Pulls live YouTube trends, and reads your written-down TikTok and Meta observations. Real numbers, never invented ones — and it labels which is which.

🧬
02 / SYNTHESIZE

Finds the pattern

Extracts why something is working, then applies that mechanism to a genuinely original idea.

🛠️
03 / PRODUCE

Writes and builds

Script, hook, shot list, assembled deliverable — ready for a human to look at.

✅
04 / REVIEW

Checks its own work

A dedicated quality pass flags anything derivative, unoriginal, or missing required disclosure.

Nothing posts on its own. Every cycle stops and waits for you to approve, reject, or edit before anything goes anywhere — that's a design decision, not a missing feature.
